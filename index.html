About this property

Some homes you shop for. This one you fall for — and it starts the moment you step into the backyard.

Behind 437 4th Avenue is a private oasis: mature trees, lush landscaping, a paver patio made for morning coffee, and a cozy gravel lounge that becomes your favorite seat the minute the sun goes down.

Then there is the barn. Nobody goes looking for a home because of a barn, until they see this one. It is the rare, flexible space most homes do not have — a workshop, an art studio, a home gym, real storage, or whatever you have been waiting for the room to build.

Inside, the home is move-in ready and freshly painted throughout, with an updated bathroom, an updated kitchen with pantry storage, and a full finished basement for bonus living space. All in the heart of Westwood, two blocks from the walkable downtown and the train, with an express bus to the Port Authority in under 35 minutes. Low taxes at just $8,812 a year.

  • The barn — a detached flex space: workshop, studio, gym, or serious storage.
  • Private backyard oasis — paver patio, gravel lounge, mature trees, deep lawn.
  • Move-in ready — freshly painted throughout; updated full bath with new flooring, vanity, sink and toilet.
  • Full finished basement — family room plus a bonus room for an office or gym.
  • Two blocks to downtown Westwood and to NJ Transit.
  • Two off-street parking spaces, a covered front porch, and a rear deck off the kitchen.
